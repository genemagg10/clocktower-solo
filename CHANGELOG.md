# Changelog

## Unreleased

A solo Bad Moon Rising storybook: random seat and character, 5–15 players, an automated storyteller, and computer neighbors.

### Storyteller choices

Where the script leaves a choice to the storyteller, this game does the following:

- The Lunatic is shown a Demon who is not the real Demon, a full evil briefing (named "Minions" and three bluffs, one of which is in play when possible), and that Demon's night choices. Those choices do not kill or poison. The real Demon learns who the Lunatic is and, when the Demon wakes, who they chose. If the Lunatic is drunk or poisoned, the Demon is not told that night's choice. A human Lunatic is not told the truth until the grimoire.
- The Lunatic's public play can give the truth away (kills that do not match, "Minions" who deny the team, the game continuing after their death). The screen itself keeps the Demon mask.
- The Assassin's kill ignores every save, including the Fool's first life and the Zombuul's first life.
- The Zombuul's first "death" is not a real death, so the Mastermind does not start and "nobody died today" can still be true. The Zombuul still counts as alive for the two-player evil win.
- Mastermind, following the How to Run: the Demon’s execution does not end the game, even if only two players remain. There is one more night and one more day. The town is not told that the Demon died. On that last day, executing a good player (even if they survive) is an evil win; executing an evil player or nobody is a good win.
- Moonchild: the curse checks whether the Moonchild is sober on the night the death would happen, and whether the chosen player was good at the moment they were chosen. The public choice does not trip the Goon. A dead Moonchild who did not actually die does not choose.
- Pukka: if the delayed death is prevented, the poison still ends that night. If the Pukka does not act, last night's victim stays poisoned.
- An exorcised Demon does not attack and does not learn the Lunatic's choice. A Po who was exorcised is not treated as having chosen nobody.
- Chambermaid counts real wakes, including a Lunatic and a drunk player, and not an exorcised Demon. She wakes last, so the number includes the rest of the night.
- Sailor: choosing yourself makes you drunk. Otherwise the other player is drunk about two times in three.
- Innkeeper: both chosen players are protected for the night even if one of them is the drunk one. The protection is the Innkeeper's, already used.
- Godfather shifts Outsiders by +1 or −1. If one direction cannot be built from the script, the other is used.
- Shabaloth returns a previous corpse about one night in three, until a long stalemate, when the storyteller stops.
- Pacifist saves a good execution a bit more than one time in three, and not when three or fewer players are socially alive.
- Tinker might die on the night sheet (about one night in nine) and, more rarely, at dawn.
- Gossip statements are structured so the game can tell whether they were true.
- If the number of living players does not fall for six days, the storyteller breaks the stalemate with one death that ignores protection, aimed at the Demon when more good players are alive and at someone else otherwise. Evil bots who are stuck will also offer a Minion.
- Vote to execute: at least half the socially alive players, rounded up. A later nominee must strictly beat the current tally. Dead players have one ghost vote. Each socially alive player may nominate once. You cannot nominate yourself.
